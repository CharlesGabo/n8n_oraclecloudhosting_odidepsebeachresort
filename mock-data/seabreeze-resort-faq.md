# Seabreeze Cove Resort — Mock FAQ

> Training data only. Seabreeze Cove Resort is fictional. Prices are in Philippine
> pesos and do not represent a real property.

## Resort overview

Seabreeze Cove Resort is a fictional family-friendly beachfront resort in San
Isidro, Batangas, Philippines. It offers overnight rooms, day-use access, a pool,
beach access, a restaurant, and small-event packages.

## Contact and service hours

- Messenger support: daily, 08:00–20:00 Philippine time.
- Reception: open 24 hours for checked-in guests.
- Mock phone: `+63 900 000 0000`.
- Mock email: `reservations@example.invalid`.
- Address: `123 Demo Beach Road, San Isidro, Batangas` (fictional).
- Normal human-response target: within two hours during support hours.

## Check-in and check-out

- Check-in begins at 14:00.
- Check-out is by 12:00 noon.
- Early check-in and late check-out depend on availability and may have a fee.
- Guests must present a valid government-issued ID at check-in.
- Minors must stay with a parent or legal guardian.

## Day use

- Hours: 08:00–18:00.
- Adult mock rate: ₱600.
- Child aged 4–11 mock rate: ₱350.
- Children aged 0–3 enter free with a paying adult.
- Day use includes pool, beach, shower, and common-area access.
- Rooms, meals, cottages, towels, and water activities are not included.
- Prior reservation is recommended; staff must confirm capacity.

## Reservations and payment

To request a reservation, collect:

1. Guest name.
2. Arrival and departure dates.
3. Number of adults and children, including children's ages.
4. Preferred room or day-use option.
5. Mobile number or preferred contact method.

A request is not a confirmed booking. Staff must verify availability and send an
official quotation. A 50% deposit is required only after staff confirmation.

Mock accepted payment methods: GCash, bank transfer, Visa, Mastercard, and cash at
the property. The resort never asks guests to send card numbers, CVVs, passwords,
one-time PINs, or complete payment credentials through Messenger.

## Cancellation and rebooking

- At least 7 days before arrival: deposit may be refunded, less payment fees.
- Between 3 and 6 days before arrival: one rebooking is allowed within 90 days,
  subject to availability and rate differences.
- Less than 72 hours before arrival or no-show: deposit is non-refundable.
- Severe-weather and government travel restrictions are reviewed by staff.
- Group bookings and events may use different written terms.

The AI may explain these examples but must refer disputes, refund approvals, and
exceptions to a human.

## Amenities

- Beach access and outdoor swimming pool.
- Complimentary Wi-Fi in rooms and common areas.
- Free parking for registered guests, subject to space.
- Restaurant hours: breakfast 06:30–10:00; all-day menu 11:00–21:00.
- Shower rooms and changing areas for day-use guests.
- Towels are included for overnight guests; day-use towel rental is ₱100.
- No gym, spa, airport shuttle, or room service in this mock scenario.

## Food and outside items

- Outside food is allowed only in designated picnic areas.
- Cooking appliances, portable stoves, and open flames are prohibited.
- A ₱300 corkage fee applies per standard-size alcoholic beverage bottle.
- Guests must not bring glass containers to the pool or beach.
- Dietary requests should be sent at least 48 hours before arrival; availability
  is not guaranteed until staff confirms.

## Pets

- One small dog or cat up to 10 kg is allowed only in designated Garden Rooms.
- Mock pet fee: ₱500 per stay.
- Pets must be vaccinated, supervised, and leashed in common areas.
- Pets are not allowed in the pool, restaurant, or Deluxe Sea View rooms.
- Service-animal requests are handled individually by staff.

## Accessibility

- Two ground-floor Garden Rooms have step-free entrances.
- The restaurant and reception have step-free access.
- Some beach areas and upper-floor rooms are not wheelchair accessible.
- Guests should describe specific access needs so staff can confirm suitability.

## Pool, beach, and safety

- Pool hours: 07:00–21:00.
- No lifeguard is guaranteed; children require continuous adult supervision.
- Swimming may be restricted during rough weather or maintenance.
- Quiet hours: 22:00–07:00.
- Smoking is allowed only in marked outdoor areas.
- Illegal drugs, weapons, harassment, and dangerous behavior are prohibited.
- For an active emergency, contact local emergency services and resort staff; the
  AI must not attempt to provide emergency or medical instructions.

## Events

- Mock packages are available for up to 80 guests.
- Event pricing depends on date, guest count, menu, rooms, and equipment.
- Collect the event type, preferred date, estimated guests, room requirements,
  contact name, and contact details, then refer the request to events staff.
- The AI must not quote a final event price or promise a date.

## Human handoff

Escalate to staff when the guest:

- asks for live availability, final quotations, discounts, or booking confirmation;
- requests a refund, cancellation approval, payment verification, or exception;
- reports an emergency, injury, safety issue, harassment, or lost property;
- has an accessibility, allergy, legal, privacy, or special medical concern;
- is angry, repeats a misunderstood question, or explicitly requests a person;
- asks something not supported by this knowledge base.

For handoff, apologize briefly, summarize the request without exposing sensitive
data, collect only the minimum contact information, and state that staff will reply
during Messenger support hours. Never claim that escalation has occurred unless
the workflow actually sends or records it.
